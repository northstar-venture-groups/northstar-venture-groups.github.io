Start with the actual work

A policy that covers dog walking may not cover boarding a client's animal in your home, grooming, obedience training or house sitting when no pet is present. Make a list of where you work, who handles animals, whether you transport them and whether you hold clients' keys. Ask the insurer to confirm each service and location in writing.

This is a reading guide, not insurance advice. A broker and the policy wording, not this page, determine coverage for a claim.

Questions to send your insurer

1
Who is insured?You, partners, employees, independent contractors and temporary helpers may have different rules.
2
Where are animals covered?Client homes, your home, a rented space, transport and off-leash areas need separate confirmation.
3
What happens to a pet in your care?Ask about accidental injury without negligence, loss, escape and per-incident caps.
4
What happens to a client's property?Lost keys and damage to the home may have distinct coverage or exclusions.
5
What proof can you give a client?Ask when certificates are issued, whether a venue can be added and what that costs.
Do not rely on a copied quoteCoverage and eligibility vary by location and services. Get a current written quote, read exclusions and make sure the listed business activities match what you will actually do.
