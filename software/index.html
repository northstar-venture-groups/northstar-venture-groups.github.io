The short answer

For one person who needs scheduling and invoicing but wants to keep costs down, compare Time To Pet Lite at $25/month with Precise Petcare Solo at $20/month. For a business that wants richer owner updates and a later team workflow, test Time To Pet Solo and Scout side by side. These are starting points, not field-tested winners.

What the pricing hides

Time To Pet: watch the tier switch

Lite is meant for one-person businesses that need basic scheduling and invoicing. Solo adds capabilities the vendor identifies for growing one-person firms, including videos, integrations and email marketing. The published Team base is $40 plus $16 per active staff member per month. Its FAQ says admins and office managers always count as active, while other staff count when scheduled in the billing period. If you run a seasonal team, test a busy and a quiet month rather than using one headcount.

Scout: compare Core and Plus

Scout lists Core at $33/month solo and $33 plus $15 per active staff for a team. Plus starts at $39/month solo and $39 plus $18 per active staff. The vendor describes scheduling, automatic payment, GPS records and photo check-ins. Do not treat those as proof that every feature is in Core: open its plan matrix and test the exact actions your business uses before committing.

Precise Petcare: flat staff bands

The vendor lists $20/month for solo and $45/month for two to five active staff. It says every tier has its complete feature set, including client and staff apps, service requests, payments, GPS and late-arrival alerts. A flat band could be easier to budget for a small team, but confirm what counts as active staff and the payment processor cost before drawing that conclusion for your own business.

A five-question fit check

1
Can a client request a changed visit?Try an extra pet, a holiday surcharge and a cancellation after approval.
2
Can staff see only what they need?Test handoff, private notes and whether an owner can view the completed visit.
3
Can you recover a bad invoice?Make a dummy invoice, edit it, refund it and export the record.
4
What is the real busy-month price?Count every active sitter, administrator and paid add-on.
5
Can you leave cleanly?Locate export options, cancellation rules and data-retention terms before importing clients.
