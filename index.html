Start with the decision

Software is worth buying when a missed visit, lost client detail or unpaid invoice costs more than the subscription. A solo sitter with only a few clients can start with a calendar, a secure intake form and a consistent invoice process. A growing team needs shared scheduling, roles and visit records.

Our comparison puts the published prices beside the operational differences, then gives a 14-day test that checks whether software actually saves time. No vendor paid for placement.
